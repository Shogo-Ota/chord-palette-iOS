## What's New — English

This update improves accompaniment quality and playback stability.

- Refined the groove and tone of Dance, Driving, Funk, and City styles

- Improved per-chord voicing-position controls
- Improved MIDI and video export consistency

## App Review Notes

Suggested test path:

1. Open the editor and enter `F | G | Em | Am`.
2. Set tempo to 120 BPM, instrument to Piano, and drums to Off.
3. Open accompaniment selection and audition Natural, Variation, and Arpeggio.
4. Long-press a chord to change its voicing position.
5. Open Export to verify MIDI/video export availability.
